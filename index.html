Event Rules
  • Event registration closes 24 hours before the event starts.
  • Paid event fees are non-refundable unless the event is cancelled.
  • Participants must provide valid contact information.
  • Each registration produces a unique QR code, valid for single entry only.
  • Participation certificates are issued only to registered attendees.
  • Volunteers must complete required training before the event.
  • Organizers must commit to the full event duration.
Volunteering
You can register as a volunteer for events that have open volunteer roles, choose a preferred role (such as registration or logistics) and an availability shift, and complete required training before the event. Volunteer hours are tracked, and a volunteer certificate is issued upon completion.

Internship Opportunities
Information below is drawn directly from the Inquisitors Society knowledge base, covering general internship rules and the 2026 Internship Program.
General Internship Rules
  • Only registered companies can post internships, and every posting requires administrative approval before publication.
  • Students can apply to a maximum of 5 internships per month.
  • Internship duration ranges from 4 weeks to 6 months.
  • Interns must submit weekly progress reports.
  • Internship completion requires mentor evaluation and company feedback.
  • Successful interns receive completion certificates from the company.

Who We Are
Inquisitors Society is a student-run society at the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore. It is dedicated to fostering technical excellence, professional development, and community engagement, through technical events, conferences, seminars, competitions, industrial tours, career development programs, leadership activities, CSS and IELTS sessions, mock interviews, and weekly discussions. Inquisitors Society is listed among UET's officially registered student societies for the 2025–2026 academic year, with Dr. Anna Niazi (Director Students Affairs) listed as advisor.

Membership
  • Students of UET Lahore and affiliated institutions are eligible for free membership.
  • External members may be required to pay a nominal registration fee determined by the society.
  • Membership is renewed annually at the beginning of the academic year.
  • Members must agree to the society's code of conduct and a valid university email is required for verification.
  • Members are required to complete their profile within 7 days of registration.
  • Alumni members may have limited access to certain features.
Orphanage Outreach Program
The Inquisitors Society Orphanage Outreach focuses on learning, games, mentorship, interaction, support, and care — creating meaningful experiences for children. Registration is via a Google Form shared in the official announcement, with a WhatsApp group also provided for participants.
